# Glossary

The words z-finance uses, in alphabetical order. Each entry points to the chapter that explains it
in detail.

**Account.** A place where money is: a current account, a savings account, cash, a card — or a
debt, which z-finance keeps as an account with a negative balance. Every account is private or
business. [Accounts and categories](accounts.md)

**Allocation.** The split of an income into shares — the levy, a debt, pots — according to your
rules. z-finance proposes it and books it only when you confirm. [Allocation](allocation.md)

**Area.** _Private_ or _business_. Every account belongs to one; the reports and the levy work
per area. [Accounts and categories](accounts.md)

**Assessment notice.** The tax office's decision on a year's income tax. It fixes the
prepayments of the following year and the back-payment of the year just ended.
[Taxes & levies](tax.md)

**Available after levies.** The second line of the forecast: your balance day by day minus the levy
gap that is still open. [Forecast](forecast.md)

**Back-payment.** What you owe the tax office after the year's assessment: the year's levy target
minus the prepayments. z-finance plans it as an expected item. [Levy & revenue](levy.md)

**Booked.** A booking that has happened; it counts towards the balance. The other states are
_planned_ and _skipped_. [Quick entry](entry.md)

**Category.** What a booking was for: groceries, rent, fees. Income, expense or transfer.
[Accounts and categories](accounts.md)

**Confirm.** Turn a planned item into a real booking, possibly with another amount or date.
[Due items](due.md)

**Counterparty.** Who you paid or who paid you. [Quick entry](entry.md)

**Debt.** Money you owe. z-finance keeps it as an account with a negative balance and, with a fixed
instalment, a recurring transfer that pays it down. [Debts](debts.md)

**Duplicate.** A line of a bank statement that has been imported before. It is shown in grey and
never written twice. [Import](import.md)

**Emergency order.** The order in which pots are emptied when money is needed at once: rank 1
first. A locked pot is never touched by it. [Savings pots](pots.md)

**Expected item.** A one-off planned item, such as an invoice you are waiting for. Unlike a
recurring item, it happens once. [Recurring items](recurring.md)

**Forecast.** The balance of your accounts day by day for the next 3, 6 or 12 months, made of
today's balance and the planned items. [Forecast](forecast.md)

**Free until the end of the month.** The money you can still spend this month: balance of the
payment accounts, minus planned items, minus reserve, minus the open levy gap. The one number of
z-finance. [Dashboard](index.md)

**Income tax prepayment.** The quarterly amount you pay to the tax office in advance (German:
_Einkommensteuer-Vorauszahlung_, abbreviated ESt-VZ). [Taxes & levies](tax.md)

**Levy.** The part of your business income that income tax and SVS will claim. It is set aside from
the day the income is booked. [Levy & revenue](levy.md)

**Levy gap.** What the levy target of the year still lacks: target minus levy pot minus prepayments.
It is subtracted from the free amount. [Levy & revenue](levy.md)

**Levy pot.** The savings pot that holds your levy reserve. [Levy & revenue](levy.md)

**Levy rate.** The percentage of business income that is levied, for example 30 %.
[Levy & revenue](levy.md)

**Overdue.** A planned item whose date has passed without you confirming, postponing or skipping it.
More than 30 days late it is marked in words and with an edge. [Due items](due.md)

**Owner draw / contribution.** Money you move between business and private. It is a transfer, so it
is neither income nor expense. [Month](month.md)

**Payoff budget.** The amount per month you put into your debts on top of the instalments.
[Payoff plan](payoff.md)

**Planned.** An item that has not happened yet: a recurring item's next due dates and expected
items. It changes no balance until confirmed. [Recurring items](recurring.md)

**Pot.** A part of an account set aside for a goal, with an optional target amount and date.
[Savings pots](pots.md)

**Profile (import).** The description of how one bank writes its CSV files: which column is the
date, the amount, the text. You set it up once per bank. [Import](import.md)

**Proposal.** The result of an allocation rule for one income, waiting for your decision.
[Allocation](allocation.md)

**Receipt agent.** An optional add-on that reads photos of receipts from a mailbox and hands them to
z-finance. [Receipts](receipts.md)

**Recurring item.** A payment that repeats — weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly. You enter it
once; z-finance plans the next twelve months. [Recurring items](recurring.md)

**Reserve.** The amount that should always stay on an account (reserve floor) or across all
accounts (total reserve). The forecast warns when the balance falls below it.
[Accounts and categories](accounts.md)

**Rule.** A category assignment for bookings whose text contains a word or matches a pattern, so
you assign it once. [Month](month.md)

**Skip.** Mark a planned item as not happening this time. It counts nowhere; the recurring item
goes on. [Due items](due.md)

**Special payment.** An extra payment into a debt, above the instalment. [Debts](debts.md)

**SVS.** The Austrian social insurance for the self-employed; z-finance plans its quarterly
contribution like the income tax prepayment. [Taxes & levies](tax.md)

**Transfer.** Money moving between two of your accounts: two linked bookings that cancel out.
[Quick entry](entry.md)

Every term here links to the chapter that explains it in full — the glossary only gives the short
version.
